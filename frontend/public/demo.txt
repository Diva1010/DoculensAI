Harbor Point Community Garden: 2026 Annual Report

Overview

Harbor Point Community Garden is a volunteer-run garden in the fictional town of Harbor Point. It opened in March 2019 and now has 64 plots shared by 112 member households. The garden is open daily from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Membership and Plots

A plot costs $45 per year for residents and $70 per year for non-residents. Each plot measures 4 meters by 2 meters. The waiting list currently holds 38 households, and the average wait for a plot is about 14 months.

Harvest and Sustainability

In 2026, members harvested an estimated 3,400 kilograms of vegetables, with tomatoes, squash, and kale being the most popular crops. The garden collects rainwater from the tool shed roof, which supplies about 60 percent of its watering needs. Compost bins turn kitchen scraps into soil for the plots.

Community Programs

The garden hosts a monthly workshop on topics such as composting and seed saving. In 2026, a youth program taught 24 local students how to grow food. The garden also donated 520 kilograms of produce to the Harbor Point Food Pantry.

Challenges and Plans for 2027

The main challenge in 2026 was a dry summer that increased water costs. For 2027, the board plans to add 12 new plots and to install a second rainwater tank. The board does not plan to raise membership fees.
